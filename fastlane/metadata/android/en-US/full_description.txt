Boice is speech-to-text that runs entirely on your device. Whether you're writing emails, taking notes, or chatting, your voice never leaves your phone — no account, no tracking.

VOICE TYPING IN ANY APP
Tap the microphone on a compatible keyboard — such as Microsoft SwiftKey — or a website's voice-search button, and your speech is transcribed straight into the text field. The app registers as your device's speech-to-text provider.
Note: some keyboards (such as Gboard) only use Google's own voice typing and can't pass speech to other apps. If yours does, use the built-in voice keyboard described below, which always works.

VOICE KEYBOARD
A dedicated voice keyboard with punctuation keys, Cancel, and live text while you speak (with streaming models).

EDIT TEXT BY VOICE
Select text, tap the wand and say what to change: "make this shorter", "translate to German".

OPTIONAL AI POST-PROCESSING
A second microphone sends the finished text — never audio — to an AI model you choose, to fix grammar, reformat or translate. Works with any OpenAI-compatible server, including a local Ollama or LM Studio. Off by default.

NOTHING GETS LOST
If the text field is gone when transcription finishes, the text is copied to the clipboard. Every dictation is kept in an on-device history with its recording.

LIVE SUBTITLES
Real-time, on-device captions for any audio or video playing on your phone.

TRANSCRIBE AUDIO FILES
Share an audio file to the app (or open it with the app) to turn the recording into text.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Recognition is 100% offline — works in airplane mode
• Private by design — audio is processed on-device and never sent anywhere
• 25 languages built in, more with imported models (Whisper, Nemotron and others)
• Free and open source (MIT License)

The speech model (~485 MB) is included in the app; nothing is downloaded on first launch.

POWERED BY OPEN SOURCE
• NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 — on-device speech recognition (GGUF by handy-computer)
• transcribe.cpp — the inference engine by CJ Pais / Handy Computer
• A native Android interface with a Rust core

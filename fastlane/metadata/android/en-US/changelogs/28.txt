- recording in the background now really records: before, Android muted the microphone once the keyboard was hidden
- a notification shows the recording, with Stop and Cancel; a vibration every 10 minutes, and it stops by itself after an hour
- without notification permission, or with "Record in background" off, hiding the keyboard stops the recording and keeps it
- recordings are saved while you speak and survive errors and killed processes
- history shows what is recognized fully, partly or not at all, and can transcribe a recording again
- long recordings and shared audio files are transcribed a minute at a time, without running out of memory; transcription can be canceled
- "Load models" works with OpenRouter's long model list
- long silence at the start and end is left out; auto-stop works in noisy rooms
- fixes from Offline Voice Input 0.2.x: model changes without a keyboard restart, safer first model unpacking, retry after an error, TalkBack, Chrome voice search, more translated texts
- debug log without dictated text, to share when something goes wrong
